Our specialists

The Shindal team consists of qualified professionals with higher education in special education, speech therapy, defectology and child psychology, each with years of hands-on experience. Every specialist regularly completes professional development courses and applies modern, evidence-based teaching methods in practice.

Our staff treat every child as an individual and take their psychological and physiological development into account. Specialists build a warm, trusting relationship with each child and run sessions in a playful, engaging and effective way. They also give parents practical advice on continuing the work at home.

All sessions are individual, one-on-one with the child — we do not run group classes for correctional work.

Speech Therapist — SLP

Specialty: correcting speech disorders. Works with both children and adults. Every session is built around the child's own pace, using play, repetition and clear modeling of sounds and words so progress feels natural rather than forced, and short home exercises are shared with parents after each visit.

Special Education Teacher (Defectologist)

Focus: correction and development work with children who have developmental needs. Works to an individual program for each child.

Psychologist

Works with children's emotional and behavioral development, and consults parents on communication and support strategies at home.

AFE and ABA Therapy

Physical movement development and behavioral therapy. A new group has opened — places are limited. NEW

Sensory Integration Specialist

Helps children process and organize sensory information from the body and environment through structured, playful activities.

Neuropsychologist

Assesses how brain function affects learning, attention and behavior, and builds a correction plan based on the findings.
