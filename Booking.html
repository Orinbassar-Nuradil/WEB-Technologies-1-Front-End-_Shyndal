Book an appointment

Booking an appointment with a Shindal specialist is simple and convenient. Fill in the online form below to request a session. Our administrators review every request quickly and will contact you by phone or messenger to confirm a time that works for you.

Please provide the parent's full name, a contact phone number, the child's age and the specialist you need. If this is your first visit to our center, we will first schedule a comprehensive diagnostic session. This helps us assess the child's developmental level and plan the following sessions correctly.
