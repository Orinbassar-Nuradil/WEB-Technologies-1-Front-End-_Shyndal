About Shindal

For six years, Shindal has helped more than 500 children develop skills for everyday life.

We offer one-to-one sessions for children with speech delays, autism, and difficulties with attention or hyperactivity. Our specialists work closely with each family.

Our Services Contact Us

Our mission

Our approach

An initial assessment guides each child’s individual program.

Our methods

Our specialists use evidence-based techniques in their sessions.

Learning spaces

Children work with their specialist in safe, specially equipped rooms.
