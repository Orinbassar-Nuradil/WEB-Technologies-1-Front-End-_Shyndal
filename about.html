About Shindal

Shindal is a development support center that helps children reach their potential. For 6 years we have worked with modern methods and an individual approach, and we have already changed the lives of more than 500 children.

Every child works with our specialists individually, following a personal program built after the first diagnostic session. We work closely with children who have speech delay, autism spectrum disorders, attention deficit and hyperactivity, and with their parents. Classes take place in specially equipped, safe rooms.
