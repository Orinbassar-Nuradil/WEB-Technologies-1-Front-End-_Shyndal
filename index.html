Welcome to the Shindal Correction and Development Center

About the center

The Shindal Correction and Development Center is a specialized institution dedicated to the comprehensive development of children's speech, thinking and behavior. For 6 years, Shindal has provided high-quality psychological and educational support and has already changed the lives of more than 500 children. Our main goal is to identify each child's individual needs and potential and to create the right environment for their development and active participation in society.

Every child works with our specialists individually, following a personal development program built after the first diagnostic session. Our team includes experienced speech therapists, special education teachers, psychologists, adaptive physical education (AFE) specialists and ABA-therapy professionals. Classes take place in specially equipped, safe rooms with modern teaching materials. We work closely with children who have speech delay, autism spectrum disorders, attention deficit and hyperactivity, and with their parents.

Our services

  • Speech therapy
  • Special education (defectology)
  • Psychology
  • Adaptive physical education (AFE)
  • ABA therapy
  • Sensory integration
  • Neuropsychology
  • Neurology consultations
